# AdSense and Content Monetization Strategy

## Principle

HelloDeploy should not be built around advertisements.

The product remains the primary value.

Recommended model:

```text
Useful technical content
        ↓
Organic discovery
        ↓
Trust in HelloDeploy
        ↓
Product usage
        ↓
Paid plans
        ↓
Advertising as secondary revenue
```

## Suitable Ad Pages

Ads may be appropriate on:

- educational articles
- long-form guides
- troubleshooting articles
- case studies
- substantial glossary pages

## Avoid Ads On

Do not place ads on:

- login
- signup
- password reset
- dashboard
- project configuration
- deployment execution pages
- deployment logs
- environment variable pages
- billing
- account settings
- error screens
- operational controls

## Internal Readiness Target

There is no required article count.

Use this as an internal quality target:

### Core Product Pages

- Homepage
- Features
- How It Works
- Pricing
- Supported Runtimes

### Trust Pages

- About
- Contact
- Privacy
- Terms
- Cookies
- Acceptable Use

### Documentation

At least 5 to 10 useful documentation pages.

### Learn

At least 15 substantial educational articles.

### Troubleshooting

At least 5 complete troubleshooting guides.

### Case Studies

At least 2 to 3 real deployment case studies.

## Originality Strategy

Prioritize future content based on real HelloDeploy usage:

- common deployment errors
- common DNS mistakes
- real framework configurations
- real domain setups
- deployment lessons
- anonymized platform statistics
- before and after troubleshooting examples
