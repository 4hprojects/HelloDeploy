Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose

HelloDeploy infrastructure must not be used in ways that harm users, systems, networks, or third parties.

## Prohibited Uses

Prohibit use for:

- malware
- phishing
- credential theft
- unauthorized access
- spam
- denial-of-service attacks
- botnet activity
- illegal content
- illegal services
- intellectual property infringement
- malicious automation
- abuse of infrastructure
- attempts to bypass resource limits
- unauthorized cryptocurrency mining if unsupported
- attacks against other systems
- hosting stolen data

## Resource Abuse

Users must not intentionally consume resources in a way that disrupts the platform or other users.

## Enforcement

HelloDeploy may:

- restrict a project
- suspend a deployment
- suspend an account
- terminate service

when reasonably necessary to:

- protect the platform
- protect other users
- prevent abuse
- comply with legal obligations

## Appeal or Contact

Provide a contact route for users who believe action was taken incorrectly.
