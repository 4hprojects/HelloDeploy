# Phase 1: Terms of Service Specification

URL:

```text
/terms
```

## Required Sections

### Eligibility

Define who may use the service.

### Account Responsibility

Explain user responsibilities for:

- account security
- credentials
- activity under their account

### Acceptable Use

Reference the Acceptable Use Policy.

### Hosted Content

Clarify user responsibility for applications and content they deploy.

### Resource Limits

Explain that plans may have limits.

Do not promise unlimited usage.

### Service Availability

Avoid guaranteeing perfect uptime unless supported by an SLA.

### Payments

Cover:

- subscription fees
- billing cycle
- payment processing
- taxes if applicable

### Cancellation

Explain how users can cancel paid plans.

### Refunds

Only include refund terms that will actually be honored.

### Suspension and Termination

Describe circumstances that may lead to restriction or termination.

### Intellectual Property

Distinguish:

- HelloDeploy intellectual property
- user-owned application content

### Third-Party Services

Explain that external services may have their own terms.

### Limitation of Liability

Use jurisdiction-appropriate language.

### Changes to Service

Explain that HelloDeploy may change features or limits.

### Changes to Terms

Explain how updated terms are communicated.

### Governing Law

Set only after confirming the legal entity and applicable jurisdiction.

### Contact

Provide a valid legal/contact channel.

## Do Not Promise

Avoid promising:

- 100 percent uptime
- permanent data retention
- permanent free hosting
- unlimited bandwidth
- unlimited compute
- unlimited storage
